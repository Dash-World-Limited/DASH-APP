These terms are between you and **DASH World Limited** ("DASH", "we") and
apply when you use the DASH app or website to order from shops, restaurants,
pharmacies and markets, or to work as a DASH vendor or rider. Your DASH
account is the same account you use on DASH World Declutters, whose own terms
also apply there.

## 1. Your account

1.1 You must be 18 or older.

1.2 You sign in with a code we send to your email. Keep access to that email
secure: anyone who can read it can sign in as you.

1.3 We ask for your phone number so riders and shops can reach you about an
order. **We do not currently verify it.**

1.4 Give us accurate details, including your delivery address.

## 2. Ordering

2.1 Shops on DASH are independent businesses. The shop is responsible for
what it sells — its description, quality, and whether it may lawfully sell it
to you. [David to confirm: whether DASH or the shop is the seller of record,

2.2 Prices, delivery fees and other charges are shown before you pay and are
set by DASH's servers at the moment you order. What you pay is the total
shown at checkout.

2.3 A shop may decline an order, for example if an item is out of stock. If
it does, you are owed a full refund, including delivery.

## 3. Payment

3.1 You pay by card, bank transfer or USSD on the secure checkout of our
payment provider, Paystack. We never see your card details.

3.2 An order is placed only when Paystack confirms your payment to us.
Reaching a "success" page is not by itself confirmation.

3.3 Cash on delivery and a DASH wallet are not currently offered.

## 4. Cancellation and refunds

4.1 You may cancel **free of charge until the shop starts preparing** your
order.

4.2 Once the shop has started preparing it, and until the rider collects it,
you may still cancel; **30% of the order is kept to cover the shop's costs**
and the rest is owed back to you.

4.3 Once the rider has collected your order, it can no longer be cancelled.
If something is wrong with it, contact support.

4.4 Refunds are checked by the DASH team and returned to your original
payment method through Paystack. We will tell you when Paystack has
processed it; your bank may take a few further days to show it. We will not
describe a refund as complete before Paystack confirms it.

## 5. Delivery

5.1 A rider collects your order and brings it to your address. Times shown
are estimates.

5.2 When your order arrives, give the rider the **delivery PIN** shown in
your app. The rider cannot complete the delivery without it. Do not share it
before the rider is with you.

5.3 Be available at the address you gave, on the phone number you gave.

## 6. Vendors and riders

6.1 Vendors and riders apply through DASH and are approved by DASH before they
can sell or deliver. DASH may suspend a vendor or rider account, with a
reason, when these terms or the law require it.

6.2 Riders share their location with DASH only while online and with the app
open, so that deliveries near them can be offered and customers can follow
their order.

Do not misuse DASH: no false orders, no abuse of shops, riders or support
