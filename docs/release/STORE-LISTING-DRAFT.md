## Full description (max 4000 characters)

DASH brings the shops around you to your door.

Browse local shops, restaurants, pharmacies and markets in [launch area],
add what you need to your cart, and pay securely. A DASH rider collects your
order from the shop and brings it to you — and you can follow it from the
shop to your door.

**What you can do**
• Browse stores and their products, and search across them
• Build a cart and see the full price — items, delivery and fees — before
  you pay
• Pay securely on Paystack's checkout by card, bank transfer or USSD. DASH
  never sees your card details
• Follow your order: accepted, being prepared, picked up, on the way,
  delivered
• Give your rider the delivery PIN at the door, so your order reaches you
  and nobody else
• See your order history

**For riders**
Approved DASH riders can go online in the same app, receive delivery offers
near them, and complete deliveries step by step. Your location is shared
with DASH only while you are online and the app is open.

**Your account**
Sign in with a code sent to your email — no password to remember. You can
delete your account from the app at any time.
